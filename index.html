Locus

An open-source health app for your wearable. Sleep, recovery, strain and the rest are worked out on your phone, from your band's own data.

Your data stays on your phone. Your health data is processed and stored on the device, and nothing is uploaded unless you switch it on. The optional Community (teams, leaderboards and chat) shares only the scores you tick, and only after you sign in.

Open source. The code is public on GitHub, and so are the releases.

Locus is an independent project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any wearable maker.
